# BMARK-004 through BMARK-020 research batch

Issue #45 research coverage is completed in this batch for the remaining 17 benchmark candidates. Each record captures only public Songsterr page metadata and is conservatively classified as `other_structured_notation` because the public pages establish structured rhythm-aware notation but do not establish Guitar Pro file format.

Together with the existing BMARK-001, BMARK-002, and BMARK-003 records, every candidate in the 20-song bank now has a metadata-only structured-reference research record.

This research is discovery evidence only. It does not establish lawful local acquisition, provenance acceptance, source hashes, chart correctness, or benchmark ground truth. Those remain separate human-review and promotion gates. No notation, commercial audio, source files, local paths, private CFSM exports, Ubisoft-derived content, live Rocksmith installation, or NoCableLauncher changes are included.
