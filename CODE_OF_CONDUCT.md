# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project welcomes constructive technical and musical collaboration. Participation should remain professional, specific, and focused on improving the software and its documentation.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- critique ideas, code, tests, documentation, and evidence rather than individuals;
- provide reproducible details when reporting defects;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when evidence changes;
- respect source rights, privacy, attribution, and third-party intellectual property;
- avoid posting private data, credentials, commercial song files, player profiles, or other material that should not be public;
- keep review discussion focused on the scope and technical merits of the contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or targeted personal attacks;
- sustained disruption, spam, or deliberately inflammatory behavior;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting malicious code, credential-stealing behavior, unsafe payloads, or deceptive test artifacts;
- pressuring maintainers to bypass CI, security, provenance, validation, source-rights, or human-review gates;
- knowingly misrepresenting third-party content as freely redistributable or project-owned.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-controlled collaboration spaces.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, hide, lock, or remove material and may restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be proportional to severity, repetition, and impact on the project or its participants.

Security-sensitive reports should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being disclosed publicly before they can be assessed.
